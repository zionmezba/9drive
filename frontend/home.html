ZDrive: one storage space across your Google Drive accounts

ZDrive is a file manager and storage gateway. Connect one or more of your own Google Drive accounts, and ZDrive combines their free space so you can upload, organize, preview, and share files from a single dashboard.

Get startedHow we use your data

Combined storage

See the total and free quota of every Google Drive account you connect in one view.

Smart uploads

Uploads go to a connected account with enough free space, into a folder named 9drive in that Drive.

Folders and search

Organize files into folders, search by name, and move, rename, or delete them.

Preview and share

Preview images, video, and documents in the browser, and create share links or invite other users.

How ZDrive uses Google Drive

When you choose to sign in with Google or connect a Google Drive account, ZDrive asks for permission to see your email address and basic profile, and to access your Google Drive. That access is used only to store the files you upload in a 9drive folder, list and sync the files in that folder, stream them back to you for preview, download, or sharing, and read your storage quota. ZDrive does not sell your data, show ads, or use it to train AI models. Details are in our Privacy Policy.
