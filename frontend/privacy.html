Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ZDrive ("ZDrive", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores, shares, and deletes information when you use the ZDrive website and service at this domain. ZDrive is a storage gateway that lets you connect your own Google Drive accounts and upload, organize, preview, download, and share files stored in them.

2. Google user data and permissions

ZDrive requests the following Google OAuth scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.profile: to read your Google email address, name, and account ID so we can sign you in and identify connected accounts.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive: to create and use a folder named 9drive in your Google Drive, upload files you choose into it, list and sync the files in it, rename, move, or delete them when you ask, stream them for preview, download, or sharing, and read your storage quota.

We only access Google Drive to perform actions you request in ZDrive or to keep your file list in sync with the 9drive folder. We do not read, index, or analyze the contents of your files, and we do not access files outside what is needed for these features.

Limited Use. ZDrive's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • We use Google user data only to provide and improve the user-facing features described above.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to others except as needed to provide these features, to comply with law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with notice to you.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising, including retargeting or personalized ads.
  • We do not sell Google user data, and we do not use it to develop, improve, or train generalized AI or machine learning models.
  • No person reads your Google user data unless you give explicit consent for specific data, it is necessary for security purposes such as investigating abuse, or it is required by law.

3. How we store and protect data

  • Account and file metadata are stored in a MySQL database on our hosting server.
  • Google OAuth tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before they are stored.
  • Passwords are hashed with Argon2. Session refresh tokens, share tokens, and preview tokens are stored only as hashes.
  • Uploaded files are streamed directly to Google Drive and are never written to our server's disk.
  • All traffic uses HTTPS. Access to the server and database is restricted to the operator.

4. How we share data

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We share it only:

  • With Google, to sign you in and to perform the Google Drive actions you request.
  • With our hosting and network providers (web hosting and CDN), who process data only to run the service.
  • With people you choose: anyone with a share link you create can view or download that file, and users you invite can access the shared file or folder.
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users and service.

5. Cookies and local storage

ZDrive does not use advertising or analytics cookies. We store your session tokens and interface preferences (such as theme) in your browser's local storage. If reCAPTCHA is enabled on registration, Google reCAPTCHA may set cookies and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy and Terms.

6. Retention and deletion

  • We keep your account data while your account exists.
  • You can disconnect a Google Drive account in Settings at any time; ZDrive stops using it. You can also revoke ZDrive's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, after which stored tokens no longer work.
  • You can request deletion of your account and all associated data (profile, encrypted tokens, file metadata, share links, invites, sessions, and activity logs) by emailing %VITE_CONTACT_EMAIL% from your account email. We complete deletion within 30 days.
  • Files stored in your Google Drive belong to you and remain in your Drive unless you delete them.

7. Your rights

You can access and update your information in the app, and you can ask us for a copy of your data, correction, or deletion by contacting us. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local data protection laws.

8. Children

ZDrive is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their data.
